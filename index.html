Common questions

What file formats do I get?

DST for most commercial machines, plus PES on request. Other formats available if your machine needs something specific — just mention it in the order notes.

Can I send my own t-shirt or hoodie to be embroidered?

Yes — pick "Embroider my own item" in the order flow. I'll confirm shipping details with you once your request comes in.

What if the stitch-out doesn't look right?

One revision is included on every digitizing order at no extra cost — I want the file to actually sew well on your machine, not just look good on screen.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, physical orders (Shop items and embroidered garments) ship worldwide with tracking.
